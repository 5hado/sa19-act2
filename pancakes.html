Fluffy Pancakes

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time:10 mins

Servings: 4

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix dry ingredients.
  2. Whisk in milk, egg, and melted butter until smooth.
  3. Heat a pan and pour batter to form pancakes.
  4. Cook until bubbles appear, flip, and cook for 1 more minute.
  5. Serve with syrup and enjoy!
