Sugar Bacon

Prep Time: 5 min

Cook Time: 15 min

Servings: 4

Difficulty: Easy

    Ingredients

  • 12 strips of thick-cut bacon
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup

    Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix brown sugar, black pepper, and cinnamon in a bowl.
  3. Lay bacon strips on the sheet and brush with maple syrup.
  4. Sprinkle the sugar mixture evenly over the bacon.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes or until caramelized and crispy.
  6. Let cool for a few minutes before serving.
